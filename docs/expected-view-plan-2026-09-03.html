Expected View: one plan for the picture, the moments and the words beside a proof

Two plans touched the same square of the board: the Expected picture becoming a real HTML replica of the app's component, and design C for how a beat's small steps and the explaining chip are shown. This is the one plan that combines them. It opens with the row as it would ship, built here in HTML from one capture of the live app, no drawn SVG anywhere; the sections after it are the standard and the phases.

1 · Decisions

What is already decided, and by whom

The human, 2026-09-03. The picture beside the photograph is a real HTML replica of the app's component with the requirement's expected values applied, not a drawing derived from a skeleton. This reverses the 2026-08-18 house-style decoupled drawing. The drawing survives only as a labelled sketch for a screen with no UI yet.

The human, 2026-09-02 and 2026-09-03 (design C). One stepper per beat over both pictures; segments named by the test's own assertions; every text once: the sentence in the words cell, the moment's name in the strip caption, the chips carry only EXPECTED and ACTUAL with the value; a Then's chip is a checklist; both pictures wear the same ring and dim; one line everywhere, ellipsis and hover instead of wrapping; nothing repeated per row.

A Focus row holds two pictures of one moment. Expected is what the requirement wants, in the app's own component. Actual is what the app did. The row exists to show their difference. Likeness is what makes the difference readable; the strip is what makes the moment findable; the words are the requirement and nothing else.

4 · The standard

How an Expected picture is made

at each momentproveVisiblerings the element, reads it, claims expected vs got; names the moment
as todayphotograph + skeletonActual frame; boxes, words, ring (ring-first walk, 0.42)
newreplica capturethe ring's region as the app's own DOM; styles diffed against defaults; sanitised; capped
new · in the pageapply the claimgot → expected on the leaf; gone → restored from the last right replica; new → marked
new · at capturegaterender the replica hidden, walk it, boxes and words must match the live skeleton
  1. Derived, never guessed. Every pixel of Expected comes from a measurement of the app or a claim the test made.
  2. Expected shows intent; Actual shows what happened. Agreeing on a passing moment, differing and marked on a failed one.
  3. Intent is authored. Every fact in a Then is a soft claim, including "appears" and "is gone"; a Then fact with no claim fails the beat lint.
  4. Likeness is measured. A replica is checked against the live skeleton at capture; a gap is recorded and the proof gate refuses it.
  5. Every text once. Sentence in the words, name in the caption, value in the chip. One line each; ellipsis and hover, never wrapping.
  6. Labels honest. Replica, sketch, text-only; stale when the text or the app moved.
  7. Bounded and safe. Node and byte caps; scripts, handlers and external URLs stripped; rendered in <iframe sandbox srcdoc> with no script.
5 · Case A

The UI exists: a replica at every moment

Scene root

The smallest ancestor of the ringed element whose box is at least 3× the ring's area and at most the viewport, walking up from the element proveVisible hands over. A ring on a row captures the row and its header band. The rest of the page is paper with the app's shell plates (sidebar, header band) so the region reads in place.

Measured today: the toolbar region is 12 KB; a whole page would be a few hundred KB per moment.

Styles

Computed styles per element diffed against a per-tag probe, grouped into classes; pseudo-elements with content materialised as spans; inline svg icons kept as the app has them.

67 nodes → 40 classes in the example above.

Fonts and images

The app's font stack kept as written; its web fonts fetched once per screen and stored beside the harvest; images as data URIs; a canvas as its own snapshot; an iframe as a plate.

Open: embed fonts or accept fallback (§11).

Sanitising

Drop script, style, link, event handlers, javascript: and external URLs; whitelist attributes; form controls keep their value as text; shadow roots walked into the light tree.

The iframe forbids script anyway; sanitising is the second wall.

Apply the claim

At capture, in the page, on a clone: the leaf inside the ringed box whose words the measured got contains takes expected (the worded wrapper around it swaps the old words too); an element the claim says is missing is restored from the beat's last right replica; a never-there element gets a marked placeholder beside the ring. Claims accumulate down the beat.

Both replicas stored, Actual and Expected; the fold needs no DOM library.

Before and after

The before replica is taken from the first moment's scene root once the beat has run; the after frame is the last moment's Expected when the beat failed, else its own capture.

Mirror-13's rule, on real markup.

6 · Case B

No UI yet: a sketch, labelled, until the first harvest

Nothing can replicate a component that does not exist. The sketch kit (today's nine archetypes matched from the Given / When / Then) keeps that job, labelled sketch · no UI yet, and it is the one place a drawing remains. Its claims are the Then's facts written as soft claims before the UI exists, so they carry straight into the first harvest, which replaces the sketch with a replica. Later, optionally, the sketch is placed inside a sibling screen's replica so even the pre-UI picture wears the product.

7 · The gate

Likeness verified by measurement

Right after capture, in the app's page, the Actual replica is rendered in a hidden iframe and walked with the same skeleton walk that measured the live page. Every box and word of the live skeleton inside the scene root must be found in the replica's skeleton within 1 px and the same text; a miss is a replica gap on the harvest. The Expected replica must also carry every claim's expected value where the claim points. npm run proof mirror refuses a harvest with a gap and the fold prints them. The sketch kit's mirror guard stays for sketches.

9 · Phases

Seven phases, each test-first, each shippable and vendored into dojostack for the real-data check

1

Capture module · spec/_replica.mjs

Self-contained like _layout-walk.mjs: scene root, style diffing, sanitising, caps; proveVisible stores .actual.html beside each skeleton. Unit-tested on the stub DOM.

Accept: every harvested moment on both boards has a replica ≤ 200 KB; a fixture with a script tag and an onclick yields neither.

2

Apply the claim · Expected replica

In-page mutation of the clone: wrong value, gone, never-there; claims accumulate; the after frame of a failed beat is the last Expected.

Accept: the demo's failing R9 shows the archived task still listed, its Undo and "To do 5" in Tsumiki's real markup; House View R3 shows "Published" in the real picker.

3

The gate

Hidden-iframe re-render and walk at capture; replica gaps on the harvest; proof mirror refuses them.

Accept: deleting a text node from a replica fixture fails the gate; every real harvest passes it.

4

The row · design C on real cells

Rename the cells; render the Expected replica in a sandboxed iframe on the paper page at the app's coordinates, stepped by the one strip; the same ring and dim on both; chips EXPECTED / ACTUAL only; the Then checklist; claimed elements tinted; the words cell sentence-first with the proved phrase underlined.

Accept: the board's own R-tests for the two names, one strip per beat, no repeated text, no wrapping in strip or chips; the example above is the reference rendering.

5

Difference marker and loupe

One marker per failed claim across the two cells; the loupe row under the cells with the ringed element alone on both sides at one scale.

Accept: a failed moment shows exactly one marker naming both values; the loupe shows the same element on both sides at the same width.

6

Authored intent lint

A beat whose Then names a fact no claim covers fails proof lint; kg-e2e and kg-deep say every Then fact is a soft claim.

Accept: the lint flags a fixture beat; both boards pass after their claims are completed.

7

Sketch in the product's chrome · optional

A no-UI sketch placed inside a sibling screen's replica.

Accept: a screen with no test shows its sketch inside the app's header and sidebar.

Order: 1 → 2 → 3 before anything reaches the board, so the first replica anyone sees is already gated; 4 and 5 together because they share the ring's geometry; 6 next; 7 when a screen without UI needs it. About two and a half weeks of staff work.
