Every clip below is the real harvest of demo/todo on board :4175 — the exact span of the run's own recording that the Actual cell plays for that moment, cropped here only to fit this page.

1 · The gesture, inside the moment

A moment used to be a still: the frame taken at the instant the check read the value. The gesture that produced it fell between two stills and appeared nowhere. Now the fold files, beside each moment, the span of the recording that ends on it — from the previous moment's anchor (or the beat's window start, where the When begins) to its own, plus a 400 ms settle — and the cell plays that span, looping while the moment is parked.

The re-encode had to go, and it is the interesting number. The committed recording used to be re-encoded to VP9 at 1280 wide. Live action needs a per-moment seek, and libvpx-vp9 defaults to a keyframe every 9999 frames — a whole run with one keyframe, so every seek landed on frame 0. Forcing -g 25 fixed the seek and destroyed the reason for the pass: on this repo's own 312-second dispatch recording it took 5 minutes and produced 26.6 MB out of a 20.0 MB source, and seven dispatch specs then timed out waiting on nested runs whose folds were encoding. Playwright's own file is already VP8 1440×900 at 25 fps with a keyframe every 5.12 s; what it lacks is the Cues index. -c copy rewrites exactly that in 0.2 s at the same byte size. Measure before spending an encode.

4 · What is honest about it

The still never leaves. It is stacked underneath, it is what npm run proof mirror composes against, and it is what a click opens in the lightbox — the video is pointer-events:none. A harvest with no recording, or a beat whose window and offsets the fold cannot reconcile, keeps its still: verified by stripping data-ev-video off the rows and rebuilding the reader — 0 videos, 5 stills, nothing invented.
The Given row stays a still. It is the context row; nothing has happened yet, so there is no gesture to play.
Slices are frozen with the recording they index, exactly as the video's own offsets are — a later video-less fold moves every window with its fresh frames and must never re-aim a recording it did not cut.
